Build an amigurumi idea

The catalog includes 83 bases, 11 mythical traits, 26 outfits, and 18 accessories.

The prompt is optional. Leave it blank and generate from the manual base, trait, outfit, accessory, and color controls whenever you prefer.

Choose one base character. Browse animals, fantasy folk, creatures, and constructs. New additions include emus, kiwi birds, oni, tengu, kappa, shadow demons, catfolk heroes, and moon spirits.

Add one mythical trait. Choose mermaid, fairy, dragon, unicorn, angel, demon, phoenix, pegasus, griffin, aquatic, or mushroom features.

Add one class or outfit. Choices include magical guardian, demon hunter, spirit swordsman, and shrine guardian outfits alongside pirate, witch, artificer, samurai, chef, astronaut, royal, explorer, and the original fantasy classes.

Add accessories. Choose up to one face, head, and neck accessory. The builder labels each choice Fits, Adjusted, or Unavailable and explains the fit. Adjusted headwear includes one measured chain-opening round, a framing round worked into those chains, and a reinforcement round—or a fitted split band—when ears, horns, or similar features cross the crown.

Preview and color it. The live concept visualizer shows the selected silhouette, feature, outfit, and accessories. Open Color the parts to set yarn colors for the body, details, wings, tail, shell, outfit, and each selected accessory. These colors carry into the generated component list and planning allowance.

Try "fluffy elf ranger with round glasses", "kiwi bird magical guardian", "oni demon hunter", or "cat mermaid". Theme words such as fluffy, cozy, happy, fierce, and whimsical are accepted, while pose and eye-shape requests explain how to finish those details manually. A prompt supports one body color and one targeted accent; use Color the parts for a larger palette.

Choose a construction style. Standard sews matching neck rings and each component’s complete attachment edge. Limited sew keeps one main neck seam and hook-joins every other part while the receiving area is accessible. No-sew prepares smaller components first, works them directly into marked rounds of a closed host as it is crocheted, and uses accessible hook joins for open hosts before stuffing and closure.

Use one base, up to one mythical trait, one outfit, and one accessory per area. Detailed anatomy, complex colorwork, poses, and exact measurements remain outside this version. Unrecognized requests stop with an explanation.

These are original modular constructions inspired by broad fantasy, anime storytelling archetypes, and beginner-amigurumi categories. They do not reproduce branded characters or published patterns.

All designs are not yet crochet-tested. Their stitch arithmetic is checked; their physical fit and proportions need a prototype.
